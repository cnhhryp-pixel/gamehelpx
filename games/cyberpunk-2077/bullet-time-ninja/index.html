Cyberpunk 2077 Bullet-Time Ninja

A fast melee build centered on Sandevistan, mobility and time dilation.

CD PROJEKT RED's Bullet-Time Ninja is a fast melee build concept centered on Sandevistan, high mobility and time dilation. The official breakdown emphasizes Reflexes and Technical Ability as core parts of the setup.

Core build identity

  • Sandevistan cyberware
  • Fast melee pressure
  • High mobility
  • Reflexes + Technical Ability focus

How the playstyle works

The build creates safe attack windows by slowing perceived time and moving quickly between targets. It works best when movement, melee range and cyberware timing are treated as one system rather than separate upgrades.

Cyberware planning

Sandevistan is the defining piece, so supporting cyberware should improve survivability, mobility or the ability to stay effective during aggressive close-range engagements.

Perk planning

Prioritize perks that improve the movement and melee loop you actually use. Avoid spending heavily in unrelated branches until the core Sandevistan playstyle already feels complete.

When this build fits

This style suits players who want direct melee combat and fast repositioning rather than a stealth-heavy or quickhack-centered approach.
