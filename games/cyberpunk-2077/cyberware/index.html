Cyberpunk 2077 Cyberware

Cyberware is a core build system, not just cosmetic chrome.

CD PROJEKT RED redesigned cyberware around build identity in Update 2.0. Cyberware now supports playstyle, carries most armor value, and is limited by a cyberware-capacity system that grows as V levels up.

What to compare

  • Cyberware capacity cost
  • Armor and survivability
  • Synergy with your primary combat style
  • Operating system choice
  • Ripperdoc upgrade path

Why this matters for builds

A useful build guide should connect perks, attributes, weapons and cyberware instead of treating them as separate systems.

Source: CD PROJEKT RED — Cyberware in Update 2.0.
