Cyberpunk 2077 Solo Build

A durable blunt-weapon and shotgun build inspired by the official Savage Slugger Solo.

The Savage Slugger Solo is built around Body and Technical Ability, with strong defense and blunt-weapon finishers. CD PROJEKT RED also highlights shotguns as a natural fit.

Build identity

  • Body + Technical Ability
  • Blunt weapons
  • Shotguns
  • Defense before raw early-game damage

Source: CD PROJEKT RED — Savage Slugger Solo.
