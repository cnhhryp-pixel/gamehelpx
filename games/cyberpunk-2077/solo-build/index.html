Cyberpunk 2077 Solo Build

A durable blunt-weapon and shotgun build inspired by the official Savage Slugger Solo.

The Savage Slugger Solo is a durable close-range build concept highlighted by CD PROJEKT RED. Its identity centers on Body and Technical Ability, using blunt weapons, shotguns and strong defensive investment.

Core build identity

  • Body as a major combat foundation
  • Technical Ability for cyberware-oriented progression
  • Blunt weapons for close-range pressure
  • Shotguns as a natural ranged complement
  • Defense and survivability before chasing maximum early damage

How the playstyle works

The build wants to survive at short range long enough to keep applying pressure. Rather than playing like a fragile burst setup, it benefits from durability, reliable movement into close distance and weapons that remain effective when enemies are nearby.

Cyberware planning

Use cyberware to reinforce the build's existing job: durability, mobility and close-range combat. Avoid filling slots with unrelated bonuses just because they are available.

Perk planning

Start with perks that support the weapons and defensive style you actually use. Once the core loop feels stable, add secondary utility rather than spreading points across too many unrelated branches.

When this build fits

This style suits players who prefer direct combat, heavier weapons and a forgiving amount of survivability rather than stealth or pure quickhack play.
