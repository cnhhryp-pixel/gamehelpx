Terraria NPCs

A town-NPC reference built around unlock conditions, services and progression relevance.

Terraria NPC coverage is useful because many town characters provide services, shops or progression-related items. Some need housing, some require boss or event progress, and some must be found or rescued.

What each NPC page should answer

  • How the NPC becomes available
  • What services or items they provide
  • Whether their inventory changes with progression
  • Useful biome or housing considerations
