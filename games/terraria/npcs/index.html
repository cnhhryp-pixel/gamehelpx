Terraria NPCs

A town-NPC reference built around unlock conditions, services and progression relevance.

Terraria town NPCs provide shops, services, progression-related items and utility. A useful NPC reference needs to explain not only who an NPC is, but how that character becomes available and why the unlock matters.

Common unlock types

  • Having suitable housing available.
  • Reaching a boss or world-progression milestone.
  • Finding or rescuing the NPC in the world.
  • Meeting an item, stat or event-related condition.

Why NPC progression matters

Some shops and services become more useful as the world advances. That means an NPC can be relevant even after the initial move-in condition has been completed.

Housing and location

Housing is part of the system, not just decoration. When planning a town, leave enough valid housing for future NPCs and think about whether you want important services close to frequently used travel routes.
