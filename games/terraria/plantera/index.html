Plantera Guide

A major Hardmode progression boss with important post-Plantera unlocks.

Plantera is a Hardmode boss encountered after the Mechanical Boss phase. Defeating it is a major progression milestone because several later-game areas and challenges become available afterward.

Preparation checklist

  • Clear enough Jungle space to move safely
  • Plan a circular or multi-level arena
  • Bring mobility suited to tight terrain
  • Enter with a loadout already tested on Hardmode bosses
