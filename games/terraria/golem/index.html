Golem Guide

A post-Plantera boss fought inside the Jungle Temple.

Golem is a later Hardmode boss connected to Jungle Temple progression after Plantera. The fight takes place in a more constrained arena than many outdoor bosses.

Preparation

  • Clear the available temple arena space
  • Bring mobility that works in a confined room
  • Use post-Plantera equipment and upgrades
  • Plan for multiple targetable body parts during the encounter
