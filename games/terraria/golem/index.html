Golem Guide

A post-Plantera boss fought inside the Jungle Temple.

Golem is a later Hardmode boss tied to Jungle Temple progression after Plantera. The fight is usually more constrained than outdoor encounters, so movement planning matters as much as raw damage.

Before the fight

  • Use post-Plantera equipment rather than rushing in with an outdated loadout.
  • Clear as much useful movement space as the room allows.
  • Bring mobility that still works well indoors.
  • Keep healing and stamina-management tools ready for sustained pressure.

Arena considerations

The Jungle Temple room can limit long horizontal movement, so vertical positioning and short repositioning tools become more important. Build around the space you actually have rather than copying an arena designed for an open biome.

Fight approach

Golem has multiple targetable parts during the encounter. Focus on staying consistent and avoiding unnecessary contact damage rather than forcing long attack strings whenever the boss is close.

After Golem

Use the Hardmode hub to decide which late-game events, equipment upgrades and progression steps matter next. At this stage, accessories and mobility upgrades often become as important as the main weapon.
