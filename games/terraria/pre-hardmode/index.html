Terraria Pre-Hardmode Progression

A clean route through early exploration and preparation before Hardmode.

Pre-Hardmode is the first major progression phase. The useful goal is not to follow one rigid route, but to improve mobility, crafting access, survivability and combat options until the world is ready for the Wall of Flesh transition.

Key categories

  • Early exploration
  • Boss preparation
  • Dungeon access
  • Crafting stations and materials
  • Hardmode readiness
