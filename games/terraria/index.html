Terraria FAQ

Where should I start if I am lost in progression?

Open Progression first, then use Pre-Hardmode or Hardmode depending on your world state. The Boss Filter helps narrow the next major encounter.

Should I use Boss pages or the Progression Checklist?

Use Boss pages for fight-specific help. Use the checklist when you mainly need to track which major milestones you have already completed.

What should the Terraria database expand into next?

Weapons, armor, accessories, crafting chains, NPC unlocks and biome-linked item categories are the strongest structured-data opportunities.
