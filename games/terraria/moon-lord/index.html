Moon Lord Guide

Terraria's final main-progression boss on current desktop, console and mobile versions.

Moon Lord is the final boss of Terraria's main progression. The encounter requires destroying the eyes in the hands and head before the core in the chest can be damaged.

Summoning

Moon Lord appears after the Celestial Pillars are defeated, or can be summoned with a Celestial Sigil.

Preparation

  • Use endgame mobility
  • Plan reliable healing and sustain
  • Bring a loadout that can keep damage uptime while moving
  • Practice avoiding the Phantasmal Deathray
