Terraria Biomes

Biomes affect enemies, loot, fishing, NPC behavior and progression.

Terraria worlds contain distinct biomes with characteristic terrain, enemies, collectible items, music and other features. Surface areas include Forest, Desert, Snow, Jungle, Dungeon and the world evil, while deeper layers contain underground variants and the Underworld.

Hardmode changes

After the Wall of Flesh is defeated, Hardmode adds or expands biome states including the Hallow and underground variants of world evil.

Why biome pages matter

Biome pages can connect enemies, resources, fishing, NPC services, keys, chests and farming setups into one searchable structure.

Source: Official Terraria Wiki — Biomes.
