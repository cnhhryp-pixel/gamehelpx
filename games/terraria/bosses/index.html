Terraria Bosses

A progression-aware boss hub that separates optional encounters from major world-state milestones.

Bosses are a central part of Terraria's progression loop. Some bosses mainly provide equipment and optional rewards, while others unlock new areas, NPCs, enemies or world states.

Major milestones

  • Skeletron opens practical Dungeon progression
  • Wall of Flesh triggers Hardmode
  • Mechanical bosses unlock later progression
  • Plantera opens several late-game areas and challenges
  • Moon Lord anchors the main endgame path

Source: Official Terraria Wiki — Bosses.
