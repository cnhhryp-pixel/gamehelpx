Terraria Hardmode Progression

Hardmode changes the world and opens the largest progression phase in Terraria.

Hardmode begins after the Wall of Flesh and introduces stronger enemies, new resources, new biome states and a much larger equipment progression curve.

Progression structure

  • Immediate Hardmode survival
  • Mechanical bosses
  • Plantera and post-Plantera progression
  • Late-game events and bosses
  • Moon Lord preparation
