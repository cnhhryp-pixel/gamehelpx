The Twins Guide

Two mechanical eyes with different attack styles and independent health pools.

The Twins are one of Terraria's three mechanical boss encounters. Retinazer emphasizes laser attacks while Spazmatism uses a more aggressive close-range pattern.

Summoning

They can be summoned with a Mechanical Eye at night.

Preparation

  • Use a long arena with strong mobility
  • Focus on one eye when that makes the fight easier to control
  • Prepare for both ranged and close-range pressure
