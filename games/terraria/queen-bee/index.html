Queen Bee Guide

An optional pre-Hardmode Jungle boss with useful rewards and an NPC unlock.

Queen Bee is a pre-Hardmode boss found in the Jungle. She is not required for main progression, but defeating her provides useful items and allows the Witch Doctor to move into a vacant house.

Why players fight her

  • Optional weapons and items
  • Witch Doctor unlock
  • Useful pre-Hardmode progression rewards
