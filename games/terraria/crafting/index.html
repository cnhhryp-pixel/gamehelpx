Terraria Crafting Guide

Understand why crafting stations and progression gates matter before searching individual recipes.

Terraria crafting combines materials into new items, often at a specific crafting station. The official wiki emphasizes that crafting is tightly connected to progression because many key materials and stations become available only after certain milestones.
