Brain of Cthulhu Guide

A Crimson pre-Hardmode boss with two distinct phases.

The Brain of Cthulhu is the Crimson counterpart to the Eater of Worlds. It can spawn after every third Crimson Heart is destroyed, or be summoned manually with a Bloody Spine in the Crimson.

Fight structure

The first phase requires defeating its Creepers before the Brain becomes vulnerable. The second phase is a direct fight with frequent teleporting.
