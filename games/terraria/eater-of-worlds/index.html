Eater of Worlds Guide

A segmented Corruption boss that splits into smaller worms as body segments are destroyed.

The Eater of Worlds is a pre-Hardmode Corruption boss. It can spawn after every third Shadow Orb is destroyed or be summoned with Worm Food in the Corruption.

What makes the fight different

Each segment has its own health. Destroying an inner segment splits the boss into smaller worms, so attacks that can hit multiple segments can be especially useful.
