Terraria Items Database

Terraria's enormous item surface is a natural fit for structured database pages.

The official Terraria Wiki organizes items into categories such as tools, weapons, ammunition, armor, crafting stations, ores, bars, accessories, potions, mounts, wings and more.

Database plan

  • Weapon categories
  • Armor and accessories
  • Crafting materials and stations
  • Potions and utility items
  • Mounts, minions and wings

GameHelpX will prioritize categories with strong search demand and clear progression relationships before expanding into individual item pages.
