Wall of Flesh Guide

The boss that permanently changes a Terraria world by triggering Hardmode.

The Wall of Flesh is the key transition between pre-Hardmode and Hardmode. Defeating it permanently changes the world state and opens Terraria's largest progression phase.

Prepare the world before summoning

  • Finish important pre-Hardmode exploration.
  • Organize storage and crafting areas.
  • Build a long Underworld fighting route.
  • Stock healing and mobility items.
  • Have an immediate Hardmode survival plan ready.

Build the fight around movement

The encounter pushes continuously across the Underworld, so a reliable route is part of the fight itself. Remove terrain that blocks movement and keep enough space to attack without getting trapped.

Do not trigger Hardmode accidentally

The world changes after the boss is defeated, so treat the summon as a deliberate progression decision rather than just another boss attempt.

Immediate Hardmode priorities

Expect stronger enemies, new resources and new biome states. Your first goal after the transition should be survivability and access to new equipment, not rushing directly into the next boss.

Use the checklist

Mark the Wall of Flesh milestone in the local progression checklist, then switch to the Hardmode guide for the next stage.
