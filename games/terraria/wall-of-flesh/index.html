Wall of Flesh Guide

The boss that permanently changes a Terraria world by triggering Hardmode.

The Wall of Flesh is the key transition boss between pre-Hardmode and Hardmode. Defeating it changes the world state and opens the largest progression phase in the game.

Prepare before summoning

  • Finish important pre-Hardmode exploration
  • Build a long Underworld fighting route
  • Stock healing and mobility items
  • Have a plan for immediate Hardmode survival

After the fight

Expect stronger enemies, new resources and new biome states. It is useful to prepare storage, mobility and early Hardmode goals before triggering the transition.
