Skeletron Prime Guide

A mechanical version of Skeletron with four weaponized limbs.

Skeletron Prime is one of the three mechanical bosses. The boss has four limbs—a vice, laser, saw and cannon—but only the head must be defeated.

Summoning

Use a Mechanical Skull at night.

Preparation

  • Use an arena with room to circle and change elevation
  • Decide whether removing dangerous limbs first makes the fight safer
  • Keep track of the head spin, when pressure increases
