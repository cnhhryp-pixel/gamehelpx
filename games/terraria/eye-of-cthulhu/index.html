Eye of Cthulhu Guide

One of Terraria's earliest boss walls and a useful benchmark for early-game readiness.

The Eye of Cthulhu is a pre-Hardmode boss and commonly one of the first bosses players encounter. It can be summoned manually at night with a Suspicious Looking Eye and may also appear naturally once early progression conditions are met.

Fight structure

The fight has two phases. The first alternates hovering, charges and Servants of Cthulhu; the second becomes more aggressive and charge-focused.

Preparation checklist

  • Use an open surface arena
  • Bring reliable mobility
  • Carry enough healing
  • Use a weapon you can aim consistently while moving
