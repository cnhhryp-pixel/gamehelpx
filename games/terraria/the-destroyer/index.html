The Destroyer Guide

A Hardmode mechanical boss and a major step in early Hardmode progression.

The Destroyer is a Hardmode mechanical boss built from many segments that share one health pool. Damaged segments can release Probes that continue attacking independently.

Summoning

It can be summoned manually with a Mechanical Worm at night and may also spawn naturally under the appropriate Hardmode conditions.
