Skeletron Guide

A major pre-Hardmode checkpoint because defeating Skeletron opens practical Dungeon progression.

Skeletron is a pre-Hardmode boss tied to access to the Dungeon. It is summoned through the Old Man at the Dungeon entrance at night.

Why it matters

Defeating Skeletron turns the Dungeon from a blocked progression area into a major source of enemies, loot and later-game preparation.

Preparation checklist

  • Build space around the Dungeon entrance
  • Prioritize mobility and consistent damage
  • Bring enough healing for a sustained fight
  • Plan your next Dungeon goals before entering
