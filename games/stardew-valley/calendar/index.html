Stardew Valley Calendar

Use the seasonal calendar to plan birthdays, festivals and time-sensitive activities.

The in-game calendar shows birthdays and festivals for the current season, and also displays upcoming weddings. A public calendar is available outside Pierre's General Store, while a farmhouse calendar can also be obtained in-game.

Why calendar pages attract repeat use

  • Birthday gift planning
  • Festival timing
  • Season-specific events
  • Bookseller and special-event checks
