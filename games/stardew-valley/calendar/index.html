Stardew Valley Calendar

Use the seasonal calendar to plan birthdays, festivals and time-sensitive activities.

The Stardew Valley calendar is useful because many activities are time-sensitive. Birthdays, festivals and seasonal events can be easy to miss if you focus only on farming tasks.

What to check on the calendar

  • Villager birthdays
  • Seasonal festivals
  • Weddings and other scheduled events
  • Time-sensitive planning before the season ends

Birthday planning

If friendship or romance is a priority, scan the season early and reserve gifts for the villagers you care about. The Gift Finder and Marriage Candidates index make this easier than checking every character page one by one.

Festival planning

Festivals can interrupt a normal workday or create unique opportunities, so check their dates before committing to a large harvesting, mining or fishing plan.

Season-end planning

Use the calendar together with the Crop Planner near day 28. A crop may look attractive in isolation but still be a bad planting decision if it cannot mature before the season changes.
