Stardew Valley Fishing Guide

The variables that determine where and when fish can be caught.

Stardew Valley fishing depends on more than location. The official wiki notes that fish availability can depend on season, weather, body of water and time of day.

Fishing checklist

  • Check the season
  • Check weather requirements
  • Check water location
  • Check time window
  • Use skill, bait and tackle to make difficult catches easier

GameHelpX will eventually connect fish pages to bundle requirements and a filterable fish finder.
