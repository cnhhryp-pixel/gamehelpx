Stardew Valley Gifts

A friendship-planning hub that connects villagers to loved, liked, neutral and disliked gifts.

Gift giving is one of Stardew Valley's main friendship systems. The official wiki maintains a detailed all-villager gift reference because preferences vary substantially by character.

How to use this hub

  • Open a villager-specific page before gifting
  • Track birthdays separately because timing matters
  • Prefer easy-to-source loved or liked gifts for repeat use
  • Avoid assuming a universal item is safe for every villager

Source: Stardew Valley Wiki — List of All Gifts.
