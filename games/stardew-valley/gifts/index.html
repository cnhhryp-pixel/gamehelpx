Stardew Valley Gifts

A friendship-planning hub that connects villagers to loved, liked, neutral and disliked gifts.

Gift giving is one of Stardew Valley's main friendship systems, but the easiest way to use it is not to memorize every preference. Build a small, repeatable gift plan around the villagers you actually care about.

Start with birthdays

Birthday gifts are especially valuable for friendship progress, so check the Calendar at the beginning of each season and reserve items for priority villagers in advance.

Choose practical gifts

A loved gift that is easy to source is more useful than a difficult item you rarely have. Pick one or two reliable options for each priority villager and keep a small reserve.

Use villager-specific pages

Preferences differ by character. Open the individual villager page before gifting when you are unsure rather than assuming a generally useful item is safe for everyone.

Gift-planning workflow

  1. Pick the villagers you want to prioritize.
  2. Check their birthdays.
  3. Choose a repeatable loved or liked gift.
  4. Reserve birthday items separately from normal inventory.
  5. Use the Gift Finder for quick comparisons.
