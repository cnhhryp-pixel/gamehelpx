Penny Gifts & Birthday

A quick friendship reference for Penny.

Penny is a Stardew Valley marriage candidate with a birthday on Fall 2. Birthday planning is useful because it gives you a clear seasonal deadline for preparing a strong friendship gift.

Loved gift examples

  • Diamond
  • Emerald
  • Melon
  • Poppy
  • Poppyseed Muffin
  • Red Plate
  • Roots Platter
  • Sandfish
  • Tom Kha Soup

Simple friendship plan

Pick one loved gift that is practical for your current farm and keep a small reserve. A repeatable gift source is more useful than memorizing every possible item.

Birthday preparation

Check the calendar at the start of the season and set the gift aside early. This avoids spending the birthday searching for an item you could have prepared several days before.

Use the site tools

The Gift Finder is useful when you are deciding which villager to prioritize, while the Calendar page helps you avoid missing birthdays during busy farming weeks.
