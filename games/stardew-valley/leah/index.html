Leah Gifts & Birthday

A quick friendship reference for Leah.

Leah lives in her cottage in Cindersap Forest and is a Stardew Valley marriage candidate. Her birthday is Winter 23, giving you plenty of time during the year to prepare a reliable gift source before her birthday arrives.

Loved gift examples

  • Goat Cheese
  • Poppyseed Muffin
  • Salad
  • Stir Fry
  • Truffle
  • Vegetable Medley
  • Wine

Easy gift planning

Pick one gift that already fits your farm or routine. Goat Cheese or Wine can make sense for farms already producing artisan goods, while Salad is easy to remember as a simple reference option.

Birthday preparation

Check the calendar before Winter 23 and keep the chosen gift separate from normal sale inventory. This is especially useful for artisan goods that might otherwise be shipped automatically.

Friendship workflow

  • Choose a repeatable gift source.
  • Check Winter 23 early in the season.
  • Use the Gift Finder when comparing several marriage candidates.
  • Use the main Gifts hub for broader friendship planning.
