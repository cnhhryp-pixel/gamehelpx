Elliott Gifts & Birthday

A quick friendship reference for Elliott.

Elliott is a Stardew Valley marriage candidate who lives in a cabin on the beach. Elliott's birthday is Fall 5, so seasonal planning is useful if you want to make steady friendship progress.

Loved gift examples

  • Crab Cakes
  • Duck Feather
  • Lobster
  • Pomegranate
  • Squid Ink
  • Tom Kha Soup

Simple friendship strategy

Choose one practical loved gift or an easy liked gift that fits your normal routine. Consistency is usually easier than collecting a different item every week.

Birthday preparation

Check the calendar at the beginning of the season and reserve a gift before the birthday arrives. Keeping the item in a dedicated chest is a simple way to avoid accidentally selling or using it.

What to do if the perfect gift is inconvenient

Do not derail your farm just to chase one difficult item. Use another suitable gift, keep talking to the villager when convenient, and save the highest-value option for a birthday or when the item becomes easy to obtain.
