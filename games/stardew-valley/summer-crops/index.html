Stardew Valley Summer Crops

Use growth time and regrowth to plan a summer field before day 28.

Summer crop planning is easiest when you separate three questions: how long a crop takes to reach its first harvest, whether it regrows, and how many days remain before the season ends.

Start with the calendar

Every normal season lasts 28 days. Before planting a large field, check the current day and count forward to the first harvest. A crop that looks profitable on day 1 may be a poor choice if planted too late.

Regrow versus single harvest

Regrowing crops become more useful when planted early enough to produce multiple harvests. Single-harvest crops are easier to reason about late in the season because you only need enough time for one complete growth cycle.

Farm capacity matters

Profit is not the only constraint. Watering time, sprinkler coverage, seed cost and how often you want to harvest can all change which crop is practical for your farm.

Summer planning note

Summer is a good time to think about repeated harvests and sprinkler efficiency. If a crop regrows, planting date matters because every missed regrowth window reduces the season's total value.

Use the calculator

Enter the current day and growth time in the Crop Planner to check whether the first harvest fits before day 28 and, for regrowing crops, estimate how many harvests remain.
