Stardew Valley Villagers

Villager pages are ideal evergreen references because schedules, gifts and birthdays create repeat search demand.

Stardew Valley villagers have routines, birthdays, gift preferences and relationship events that create repeat planning needs throughout a save. This hub organizes those questions into quick-reference pages and tools.

What a villager page should answer

  • Where the villager is commonly found.
  • Birthday and calendar timing.
  • Loved or practical gift options.
  • Marriage-candidate status where relevant.
  • Useful relationship or schedule notes.

Start with birthdays

If friendship is your priority, scan the Calendar at the start of each season and reserve gifts for the villagers you care about most.

Use the Gift Finder

The Gift Finder is useful when you are comparing marriage candidates and want a quick birthday and gift reference without opening every character page manually.

Marriage candidates

The Marriage Candidates index collects all 12 romanceable villagers in one place, while individual pages provide more focused gift-planning guidance.
