Stardew Valley Villagers

Villager pages are ideal evergreen references because schedules, gifts and birthdays create repeat search demand.

Stardew Valley villagers have daily routines that can change with time, weather and location. They can provide quests, and many can receive gifts to increase friendship.

What each villager page should answer

  • Where they are commonly found
  • Birthday and calendar links
  • Loved, liked and disliked gifts
  • Marriage-candidate status where relevant
  • Related quests and events

Source: Stardew Valley Wiki — Villagers.
