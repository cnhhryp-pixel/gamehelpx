Maru Gifts & Birthday

A quick friendship reference for Maru.

Maru lives at 24 Mountain Road and is a Stardew Valley marriage candidate. Her birthday is Summer 10, so players who want to prioritize her can prepare a strong gift early in the season.

Loved gift examples

  • Battery Pack
  • Cauliflower
  • Cheese Cauliflower
  • Diamond
  • Gold Bar
  • Iridium Bar
  • Pepper Poppers
  • Rhubarb Pie
  • Strawberry

Pick a practical gift route

Several of Maru's loved gifts come from mining, farming or processing systems, so choose the one that matches what your current save is already doing. This is easier than building a whole new production chain for one gift.

Birthday preparation

Because Summer 10 arrives fairly early in the season, set aside the gift before Summer begins or during the first week. This avoids missing the date while focusing on crops and watering.

Friendship workflow

  • Choose one repeatable gift source.
  • Reserve the birthday gift in advance.
  • Use the Gift Finder when comparing candidates.
  • Use the Calendar page to plan the rest of the season.
