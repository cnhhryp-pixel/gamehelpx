Stardew Valley FAQ

Where should I start for farming help?

Use Crops plus the seasonal crop pages, then open the Crop Planner when you need to check whether a crop can finish before day 28.

Where should I start for friendship and romance?

Open Marriage Candidates or the Gift Finder, then jump to the individual villager page for birthday and gift details.

What evergreen tools are most useful here?

Crop Planner, Bundle Checklist and Gift Finder solve repeat planning tasks and are more reusable than one-off articles.
