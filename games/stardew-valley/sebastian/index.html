Sebastian Gifts & Birthday

A quick friendship reference for Sebastian.

Sebastian lives with Robin's family and is a marriage candidate. His birthday is Winter 10. Birthday gifts have a much larger friendship effect than normal gifts.

Planning tip

Use the birthday calendar together with the main gift hub so you can prepare a loved gift before Winter 10.
