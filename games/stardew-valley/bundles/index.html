Stardew Valley Community Center Bundles

Plan Community Center progress room by room without losing track of seasonal items.

Bundles are item donations made through the Community Center's golden scrolls. Completing individual bundles gives rewards, and finishing all bundles in a room unlocks a larger room reward.

Why a checklist is useful

Many bundle items are seasonal or location-specific, so missing one can delay completion. GameHelpX will turn this structure into a local browser checklist.
