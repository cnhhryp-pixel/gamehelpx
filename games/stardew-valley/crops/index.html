Stardew Valley Crops by Season

The core seasonal rules to know before building crop-profit and planting calculators.

Most Stardew Valley crops are seasonal. A season lasts 28 days, and crops that do not grow in the next season normally wither when the season changes.

Multi-season exceptions

The official wiki lists Ancient Fruit, Coffee Bean, Corn, Sunflower and Wheat among crops that can continue across their specified seasons.

Planning checklist

  • Count remaining days before planting.
  • Check whether the crop regrows after harvest.
  • Leave time for the final harvest before the season changes.
  • Use multi-season crops when they fit a longer planting plan.
