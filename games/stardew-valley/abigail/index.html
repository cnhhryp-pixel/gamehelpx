Abigail Gifts & Birthday

A quick friendship reference for Abigail.

Abigail lives in Pelican Town at Pierre's General Store and is a marriage candidate. Her birthday is Fall 13, making that day especially valuable for friendship progress if you bring a loved gift.

Loved gift examples

  • Amethyst
  • Banana Pudding
  • Blackberry Cobbler
  • Chocolate Cake
  • Pufferfish
  • Pumpkin
  • Spicy Eel

Easy planning approach

Choose one or two loved gifts that are realistic for your current farm and season, then keep them reserved instead of trying to memorize every possible item. Amethyst is a useful option to remember if mining already fits your routine.

Birthday strategy

Mark Fall 13 on your seasonal plan before Fall begins. If your preferred gift requires cooking, fishing or mining, prepare it in advance so the birthday does not become a last-minute search.

Friendship workflow

  • Check the calendar at the start of the season.
  • Pick a repeatable gift source.
  • Use the Gift Finder if you are comparing several marriage candidates.
  • Open the main Gifts hub when you need broader gift rules.
