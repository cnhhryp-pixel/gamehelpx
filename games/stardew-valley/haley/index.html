Haley Gifts & Birthday

A quick friendship reference for Haley.

Haley lives at 2 Willow Lane in Pelican Town and is a marriage candidate. Her birthday is Spring 14, so players who want early friendship progress can plan for her before the first month is half over.

Loved gift examples

  • Coconut
  • Fruit Salad
  • Pink Cake
  • Sunflower

Early-game planning

Some of Haley's loved gifts are easier to obtain later than others, so do not force a difficult gift route just because an item appears on a list. It is often better to use liked gifts early and save an easy loved gift for her birthday.

Season planning

Because her birthday is in Spring, add it to your first-season checklist if Haley is one of your priority villagers. This prevents the common problem of noticing the birthday only after the day has passed.

Friendship workflow

  • Check Spring 14 before planning the week.
  • Use a repeatable gift source when possible.
  • Keep birthday items separate from ordinary inventory.
  • Use the Gift Finder when deciding which villager to prioritize.
