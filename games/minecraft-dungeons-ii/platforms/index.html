Minecraft Dungeons II Platforms

Minecraft Dungeons II is announced for Steam, Xbox Series X|S, PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2.

Mojang lists Steam, Xbox Series X|S, PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2 for Minecraft Dungeons II, which launched on September 29, 2026.

PC and Xbox

PC players can use Steam, while Xbox Series X|S is officially supported. Xbox's launch coverage also describes Xbox on PC, cloud and Game Pass availability.

PlayStation and Nintendo

PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2 are included in Mojang's official platform list.
