Baldur's Gate 3 Cleric Build

A support and spellcasting build-planning hub for healing, buffs, control and frontline utility.

Cleric can cover healing, buffs, control, damage and even frontline duties depending on subclass and equipment, so the useful starting point is the role your party is missing.

Planning checklist

  • Define the party role first
  • Choose feats around the role, not in isolation
  • Decide whether staying single-classed is part of the plan
  • Use the party planner to check what the rest of the group covers
