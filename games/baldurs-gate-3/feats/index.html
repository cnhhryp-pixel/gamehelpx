Baldur's Gate 3 Feats

A build-oriented reference for deciding when a feat should support damage, defense, utility or role-play.

Feats are one of BG3's key character-customization decisions. Larian's early character-creation material notes that characters gain access to feat choices as they level, while the full game expands those decisions across a much larger class and subclass system.

How we organize feat coverage

  • Damage-focused feats
  • Defense and survivability
  • Spellcasting support
  • Mobility and utility
  • Build-specific feat priorities

Rather than rank every feat in one universal tier list, GameHelpX will connect feats to specific builds and class goals.

Source: Larian character creation update.
