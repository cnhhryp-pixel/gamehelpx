Wyll Guide

A companion reference for Wyll.

Wyll is an Origin companion whose default class is Warlock. His default setup naturally supports ranged magical pressure, Charisma-based dialogue checks and flexible utility.

Best role for Wyll

Wyll works well when your party wants a ranged caster who can still contribute consistent damage without using a large spell slot on every turn.

When Wyll fits your party

  • You want Charisma-based social utility.
  • You need ranged magical pressure.
  • You are considering a Warlock multiclass.
  • Your frontline and healing roles are already covered.

Build direction

Decide first whether you want him to remain a dedicated Warlock or become a multiclass character. That choice changes how valuable deeper Warlock levels are and how you spend the limited level budget.

Common mistake

Do not multiclass only because a popular combination exists. Make sure the second class solves a real party need or strengthens the exact role you want Wyll to perform.
