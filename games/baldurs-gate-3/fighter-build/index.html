Baldur's Gate 3 Fighter Build

A martial build-planning hub for sustained weapon pressure and frontline reliability.

Fighter is a flexible martial base for players who want straightforward weapon-focused combat, durable frontline presence, or a clean platform for subclass-specific planning.

Planning checklist

  • Define the party role first
  • Choose feats around the role, not in isolation
  • Decide whether staying single-classed is part of the plan
  • Use the party planner to check what the rest of the group covers
