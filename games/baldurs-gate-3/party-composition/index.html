Baldur's Gate 3 Party Composition

Think in party roles first, then decide which classes or companions fill them.

BG3 gives players many ways to build a four-character party. Because classes can overlap in what they provide, role coverage is often more useful than chasing one fixed “best party.”

Useful roles to cover

  • Frontline durability
  • Single-target damage
  • Area damage or crowd control
  • Healing or defensive support
  • Skills, locks and exploration utility

Use the local Party Role Planner to identify obvious gaps before choosing specific classes or companions.
