Lae'zel Guide

A companion reference for Lae'zel.

Lae'zel is an Origin companion whose default class is Fighter. Her default setup makes her a straightforward frontline martial option.

Party role

She fits parties that need weapon damage, frontline pressure and a durable martial character. Her class can be changed later through Withers.
