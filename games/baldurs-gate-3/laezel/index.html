Lae'zel Guide

A companion reference for Lae'zel.

Lae'zel is an Origin companion whose default class is Fighter. That gives her a straightforward martial role with strong weapon damage, frontline pressure and flexible subclass options.

Best role for Lae'zel

She fits parties that want a reliable martial character without adding another complicated spellcasting resource system.

When Lae'zel fits your party

  • You need a frontline weapon user.
  • Your party lacks sustained physical damage.
  • You want a companion who can use many weapon and armor options.
  • You prefer a clear combat role that leaves utility to other party members.

Build direction

Choose whether she should prioritize single-target damage, durability or battlefield control, then select subclass, feats and equipment that reinforce the same goal.

Common mistake

Do not evaluate a martial character only by one attack. Turn economy, positioning, action availability and equipment all matter over a full encounter.
