Baldur's Gate 3 Wizard Build

A spellcasting build-planning hub for broad spell access, control and ranged utility.

Wizard planning is mostly about deciding which spells and school-specific tools support the party's actual needs, rather than trying to prepare every possible spell at once.

Planning checklist

  • Define the party role first
  • Choose feats around the role, not in isolation
  • Decide whether staying single-classed is part of the plan
  • Use the party planner to check what the rest of the group covers
