Karlach Guide

A companion reference for Karlach.

Karlach is an Origin companion whose default class is Barbarian. Her default identity is aggressive frontline melee built around Rage and strong close-range pressure.

Best role for Karlach

She is a strong fit when your party needs a durable melee attacker who can stay close to enemies and create space for less durable companions.

When Karlach fits your party

  • You need a dependable frontline character.
  • Your party has enough ranged damage but lacks melee pressure.
  • You want a simple, direct combat role.
  • Your support characters need someone else to hold enemy attention.

Build direction

Focus on the role first: frontline durability, heavy melee damage or a hybrid approach. Then choose feats and equipment around that role instead of stacking unrelated bonuses.

Common mistake

Do not assume that a frontline build must wear the heaviest possible gear. Movement, initiative and positioning can be as important as raw defenses depending on the encounter.
