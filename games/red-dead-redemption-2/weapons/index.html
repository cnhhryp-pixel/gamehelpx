Red Dead Redemption 2 Weapons

A story-mode weapon reference designed to branch into individual weapon and acquisition pages.

Red Dead Redemption 2 uses several weapon categories, and the useful choice depends on range, handling, ammunition and the kind of encounter you are preparing for. This hub is designed to branch into individual weapon and acquisition pages.

Main weapon categories

  • Revolvers and pistols
  • Repeaters and rifles
  • Shotguns
  • Long-range weapons
  • Special and unique weapons

How to compare weapons

Do not look at one stat alone. A weapon that is strong at long range may be less convenient in a close encounter, while a faster-handling option may be more practical during general exploration.

Acquisition matters

A useful weapon page should explain not only what the weapon does, but also when it becomes available, how it is obtained and whether a mission or story stage affects access.

Customization and maintenance

Weapon performance also depends on maintenance and upgrades, so future individual pages will connect acquisition, customization and practical use instead of treating weapons as static database entries.
