Red Dead Redemption 2 Weapons

A story-mode weapon reference designed to branch into individual weapon and acquisition pages.

Rockstar added several weapons to the PC story mode, including the M1899 Pistol, Evans Repeater, High Roller Revolver and Le Mat Revolver.

Database structure

  • Revolvers and pistols
  • Repeaters and rifles
  • Shotguns
  • Long-range weapons
  • Special and unique weapons

Each weapon page can later connect acquisition, stats, customization and mission requirements.

Source: Rockstar Games.
