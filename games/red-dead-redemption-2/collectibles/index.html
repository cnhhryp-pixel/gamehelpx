A long-tail hub for the exploration and completion content players return to years after release.

Cigarette cards

Rockstar's own Collector's Box description confirms that cigarette cards can be found throughout the world and are organized into themed sets such as Famous Gunslingers and Outlaws, Stars of the Stage and Marvels of Travel and Locomotion.
