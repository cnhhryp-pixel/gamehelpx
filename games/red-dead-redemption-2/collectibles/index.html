Red Dead Redemption 2 Collectibles

A long-tail hub for the exploration and completion content players return to years after release.

Red Dead Redemption 2 is especially strong for evergreen completion content because players often return to its open world long after finishing the main story. Collectibles are easier to manage when they are split into separate sets and routes instead of one giant checklist.

Main collectible directions

  • Cigarette card sets
  • Treasure hunts
  • Exploration discoveries
  • Completion-oriented location pages
  • Challenge-linked discoveries

Work by region or set

Choose one collection goal at a time. Completing one card set or one treasure chain is easier to track than bouncing between unrelated collectibles across the whole map.

Use landmarks and route context

Location pages are more useful when they describe nearby landmarks and travel routes, not only a single point. That makes a guide easier to follow even when you approach from another direction.

Separate collectibles from challenges

Some exploration goals overlap with challenge progression, but they are not the same system. Use the Challenges hub and checklist when the objective is a formal challenge category, and this hub when the goal is a collectible or treasure route.
