Red Dead Redemption 2 Horses

A mount reference for story-mode horses, breed pages and acquisition guides.

Horses are a core travel and exploration system in Red Dead Redemption 2. Rockstar's PC content additions included mounts such as the Warped Brindle Arabian, Few Spot Appaloosa, Perlino Andalusian and Red Chestnut Arabian.
