Red Dead Redemption 2 Horses

A mount reference for story-mode horses, breed pages and acquisition guides.

Horses are a central travel, exploration and combat-support system in Red Dead Redemption 2. A useful horse reference should help players compare what a mount is good at, how it handles, and where it fits into normal story-mode play.

What matters when comparing horses

  • Handling: how responsive the horse feels during turns and fast movement.
  • Speed and acceleration: useful for long travel and quick repositioning.
  • Health and stamina: important for sustained travel and dangerous situations.
  • Availability: whether the horse can be found, purchased or obtained at your current story stage.

Choose for your playstyle

A horse that looks strongest on paper may not feel best in normal play. If you spend most of your time exploring and hunting, reliability and handling can matter more than chasing one maximum stat.

Story-mode planning

Mount availability changes as the story progresses, so acquisition timing matters. When GameHelpX expands this section, breed and location pages will connect directly back to story-stage guidance.
