Red Dead Redemption 2 Challenges

Challenges are ideal long-tail checklist content because players often return to finish them after the story.

Red Dead Redemption 2's story-mode challenges span multiple playstyles, so they work best as a long-term completion project rather than something you force all at once.

Challenge categories

  • Bandit
  • Explorer
  • Gambler
  • Herbalist
  • Horseman
  • Master Hunter
  • Sharpshooter
  • Survivalist
  • Weapons Expert

How to approach them

Group challenges by what you are already doing. Hunting and exploration challenges are easier to progress while roaming the world, while weapon or gambling categories may need more deliberate sessions.

Track progress separately

Use the browser checklist for high-level category tracking, then open category-specific guides when you need exact task requirements or route help.

Completion planning

Do not mix every collectible, treasure hunt and challenge into one giant list. Keeping categories separate makes it easier to see what is complete and what still needs focused work.
