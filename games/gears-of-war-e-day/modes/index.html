Gears of War: E-Day Campaign, Co-op & Multiplayer Modes

Officially confirmed campaign, co-op, Horde Siege and PvP details for Gears of War: E-Day.

Xbox currently describes an origin-story campaign starring Marcus Fenix and Dominic Santiago, plus both co-op and multiplayer options.

Campaign co-op

The campaign can be played solo. Xbox also lists two-player split-screen co-op on console and online campaign co-op for up to four players.

Horde Siege and Versus

Horde Siege is described as an evolved PvE take on the series’ Horde formula, while Versus provides PvP play.
