Gears of War: E-Day Release Date & Early Access

Gears of War: E-Day releases October 6, 2026, with up to five days of Early Access for Premium Edition players.

Gears of War: E-Day releases October 6, 2026. Xbox says Premium Edition players can receive up to five days of Early Access beginning October 1.

Platforms

The game is announced for Xbox Series X|S and Windows PC, including Steam. It is also scheduled for day-one availability through eligible Game Pass plans.
