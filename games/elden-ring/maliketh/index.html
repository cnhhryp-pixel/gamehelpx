Maliketh, the Black Blade Guide

A fast late-game main boss with a major phase change.

Maliketh is encountered in Crumbling Farum Azula and represents one of the sharpest speed increases in the late game.

Preparation

  • Practice phase-one spacing rather than rushing
  • Save resources for the faster second phase
  • Use cover and arena geometry when it helps break pressure
  • Prioritize survivability if your build is being deleted by short combos
