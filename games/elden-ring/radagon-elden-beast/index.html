Radagon & Elden Beast Guide

The final main-progression encounter is a back-to-back two-boss sequence.

The final encounter requires fighting Radagon and then Elden Beast without a normal reset between them. Resource management matters as much as raw damage.

Preparation

  • Enter with a plan for both melee pressure and long-distance repositioning
  • Preserve healing during Radagon when possible
  • Bring damage that stays reliable against both phases
  • Expect significant movement during the Elden Beast phase
