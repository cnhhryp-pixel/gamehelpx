Sorceress Sellen Questline Guide

Track Sellen's major quest checkpoints and keep the late choice separate from early sorcery progression.

Sorceress Sellen's questline begins as an early sorcery connection and later expands into a longer story involving primeval sorcerers, Witchbane Ruins and Raya Lucaria Academy.

Waypoint Ruins

Meeting Sellen at Waypoint Ruins establishes the questline and her role as a sorcery teacher. Keep this early relationship separate from the later body-transfer and Academy steps.

Primeval sorcerers

The middle of the route connects to powerful sorcerers found elsewhere in the world. Record which one you have already located so the quest does not become a vague “find another sorcerer” task.

Witchbane Ruins

Later steps involve Sellen's situation at Witchbane Ruins and progression that can move the storyline toward a new location.

Academy finale

The quest eventually returns to Raya Lucaria and presents a meaningful late choice. Treat that decision deliberately because the route and rewards differ.

Why this quest is easy to lose

It spans several regions and changes context dramatically. A one-line note such as “Waypoint → primeval sorcerers → Witchbane → Academy” is enough to preserve the structure.
