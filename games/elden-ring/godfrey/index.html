Godfrey, First Elden Lord Guide

A late-game main boss built around close-range pressure and shockwave timing.

Godfrey is a late-game main-progression boss built around close-range pressure, delayed attack timing and large shockwave-style attacks. The fight rewards disciplined movement more than panic rolling.

What makes the fight difficult

Many attacks create pressure through timing rather than pure speed. Rolling too early can put you directly into the follow-up, while overcommitting to a long punish can leave you exposed to the next sequence.

Preparation checklist

  • Keep enough Vigor to survive mistakes.
  • Use a weapon or spell setup that can punish short openings.
  • Preserve stamina so you can attack and still escape the next sequence.
  • Practice the timing of large stomp and shockwave attacks.

Second-half pressure

The encounter becomes more aggressive as it progresses, so resource management matters. Avoid using every flask or high-cost resource too early if you are consistently reaching the later part of the fight.

Build adjustment

If you are struggling, change one thing at a time: survivability, weapon speed, range, stamina management or talisman setup. This makes it easier to tell what actually improves your attempts.
