Godfrey, First Elden Lord Guide

A late-game main boss built around close-range pressure and shockwave timing.

Godfrey is a late-game mandatory encounter whose attacks reward disciplined spacing and timing rather than panic rolls.

Preparation

  • Learn the timing of large stomp shockwaves
  • Keep stamina available for chained pressure
  • Expect the fight to become more aggressive as it progresses
  • Use fast punish windows instead of overcommitting
