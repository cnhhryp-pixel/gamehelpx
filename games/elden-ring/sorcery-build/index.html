Elden Ring Sorcery Build

A spell-focused planning hub that treats FP, survivability and casting tools as one build.

A sorcery-focused character needs more than raw Intelligence. FP management, survivability, casting tools and spell selection all shape how the build feels.

Build structure

  • Primary casting stat
  • Mind for FP budget
  • Vigor for survivability
  • Staff and spell selection
  • Talismans that support casting or defense
