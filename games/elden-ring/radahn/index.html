Starscourge Radahn Guide

A major Great Rune route and one of Elden Ring's most distinctive large-scale fights.

Starscourge Radahn is one of the bosses that can provide a Great Rune for main progression. His encounter is presented as a festival battle and supports summoning multiple allied fighters in the arena.

Preparation

  • Expect strong long-range pressure at the start
  • Use the large arena instead of fighting as if it were a small boss room
  • Take advantage of summon signs when useful
  • Keep mobility high for the later stages of the fight
