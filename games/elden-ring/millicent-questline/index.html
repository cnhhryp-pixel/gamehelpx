Millicent Questline Guide

Follow the major Millicent route checkpoints and keep the late-game choice separate from the early Caelid steps.

Millicent's questline starts in Caelid with Gowry and later travels through Altus Plateau, the Mountaintops and the Haligtree.

Checkpoint 1: Gowry and the needle

Speak with Gowry at his shack, then obtain the Unalloyed Gold Needle from Commander O'Neil and return it so the quest can continue.

Checkpoint 2: Church of the Plague

Take the repaired needle to Millicent at the Church of the Plague and continue her dialogue until the Caelid section is resolved.

Checkpoint 3: Valkyrie's Prosthesis

The Shaded Castle contains the Valkyrie's Prosthesis, an important item for advancing Millicent's route when she appears in Altus Plateau.

Checkpoint 4: Later regions

Continue following Millicent through later locations, including Windmill Village, the Mountaintops and eventually the Haligtree.

Final choice

Near the end, the quest presents a branching choice involving whether to assist or challenge Millicent. Treat this as a deliberate decision because the rewards differ.

Why a separate tracker helps

This quest is spread across distant areas. Record the last location and whether the prosthesis or needle step is complete before moving on to another storyline.
