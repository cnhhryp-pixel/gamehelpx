Elden Ring Weapons

A structured hub for weapon families, build fit, weapon skills and upgrade progression.

Elden Ring's combat system lets players pair many weapon types with skills and build stats. Bandai Namco describes weapon skills as a major way to expand combat options, while later content adds more weapons and equipment.
