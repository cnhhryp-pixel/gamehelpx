Elden Ring Bosses & Progression

A route-planning hub for major encounters without pretending Elden Ring has only one correct path.

Elden Ring is an open-world action RPG, so boss progression is deliberately less linear than in many RPGs. Some encounters are central to major progression, while many others are optional and reward exploration, equipment or story.

How to use this hub

  • Check whether a boss is required for your current route.
  • Prepare damage type, defenses and upgrade level before a difficult wall.
  • Use region and dungeon pages to avoid mixing optional bosses with main progression.

Coverage structure

GameHelpX will split this into major-boss pages, optional-boss pages, region routes and build-specific preparation so users can land on the answer they actually searched for.
