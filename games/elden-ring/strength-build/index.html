Elden Ring Strength Build

A build-planning page for heavy-hitting melee and sturdy equipment.

Strength-focused builds usually revolve around heavy melee weapons and equipment. This hub separates weapon requirements, survivability, Endurance and talisman choices instead of pretending one setup is universally best.

Build checklist

  • Meet the requirements of the weapon you actually use
  • Keep enough survivability for the content you are attempting
  • Plan Endurance around armor and weapon weight
  • Choose talismans that support damage, stamina or defense
