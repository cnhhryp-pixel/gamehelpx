Fire Giant Guide

A large late-game main boss in the Mountaintops of the Giants.

The Fire Giant is a required late-game encounter on the route through the Mountaintops. Its scale makes camera control, positioning and sustained mobility especially important.

Preparation

  • Use Torrent where appropriate to reposition
  • Stay patient during long movement phases
  • Bring enough healing for a long attempt
  • Use a build with reliable damage uptime instead of only short burst windows
