Margit, the Fell Omen Guide

An early major boss that tests whether your build and upgrades are ready for Stormveil progression.

Margit is one of the earliest major bosses on the Stormveil route. He is an important progression check because beating him opens the way toward Godrick the Grafted.

Preparation

  • Upgrade the weapon you actually plan to use
  • Invest enough in survivability that mistakes are not instantly fatal
  • Learn delayed attack timing instead of panic rolling
  • Use summons if your build benefits from extra breathing room
