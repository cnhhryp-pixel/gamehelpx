Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon Guide

A major Liurnia boss with a very different first and second phase.

Rennala is encountered in Raya Lucaria Academy after the Red Wolf of Radagon. The encounter has two distinct phases, so preparation needs to account for both the first-phase puzzle structure and the second-phase sorcery duel.

Preparation

  • Do not waste resources during the first-phase puzzle
  • Save healing and FP for phase two
  • Use movement to avoid long-range magic pressure
  • Carry enough damage to punish openings quickly
