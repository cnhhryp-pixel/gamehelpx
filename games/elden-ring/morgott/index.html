Morgott, the Omen King Guide

A major Leyndell progression boss that blocks the route beyond the capital.

Morgott is a major main-progression boss encountered in Leyndell. The fight builds on the delayed timing and aggressive pressure seen earlier in the game.

Preparation

  • Arrive with a well-upgraded primary weapon
  • Keep enough Vigor to survive extended combos
  • Do not roll early into delayed attacks
  • Use openings after larger attack strings rather than forcing trades
