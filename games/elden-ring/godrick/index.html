Godrick the Grafted Guide

A key Stormveil boss and one of the early Great Rune routes.

Godrick the Grafted is a key Stormveil boss and an early Great Rune route. The fight tests whether your weapon upgrades, survivability and timing are keeping pace with the game's first major legacy-dungeon progression.

Preparation checklist

  • Arrive with an upgraded primary weapon.
  • Keep enough Vigor to survive multi-hit mistakes.
  • Use the arena to create space during large attack strings.
  • Bring enough healing for a longer attempt.

First-phase approach

Focus on learning the rhythm of larger attack strings and punish after the sequence is actually finished. Greedy extra hits are a common reason early attempts fall apart.

Phase change

The later part of the fight changes the pressure pattern significantly. Save enough healing and stamina to adapt instead of spending every resource during the opening half.

Build adjustment

If the fight is taking too long, check weapon upgrade level and damage uptime. If you are dying too quickly, survivability is more urgent than squeezing out a small amount of extra damage.
