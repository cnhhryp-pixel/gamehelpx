Godrick the Grafted Guide

A key Stormveil boss and one of the early Great Rune routes.

Godrick the Grafted follows Margit on the Stormveil route and is one of the bosses that can provide a Great Rune for main progression.

Preparation

  • Arrive with an upgraded primary weapon
  • Use the arena to create space during large attack strings
  • Expect the fight to change significantly in its later phase
  • Keep enough healing for a longer attempt
