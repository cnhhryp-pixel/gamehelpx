Elden Ring Talismans

A build-support reference for matching talismans to the role your character is trying to perform.

Talismans are one of the easiest places to fine-tune an Elden Ring build without changing the core weapon or spell setup.

Useful comparison categories

  • Damage support
  • Defense and survivability
  • Stamina and resource management
  • Spell and skill support
  • Exploration and utility

This hub is designed to branch into individual talisman pages and build-specific recommendations as the database expands.

Reference: Official Elden Ring site.
