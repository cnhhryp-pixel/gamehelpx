AION 2 Global Release Date & Early Access

AION 2 launches globally on October 5, 2026, with global Early Access beginning September 30 for eligible players.

AION 2’s global release date is October 5, 2026. NC says the global PC launch will cover North America, South America, Europe and Asia. Global Early Access begins September 30.

What platforms will the global version use?

The global version is planned for PC through Steam and NC’s PURPLE platform.

Why this page will change after launch

Once servers open globally, this page will link to server status, beginner progression, class coverage, settings and troubleshooting pages instead of duplicating those guides here.
