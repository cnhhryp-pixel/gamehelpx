AION 2 Global Platforms: Steam & PURPLE

The AION 2 global version is a PC release distributed through Steam and PURPLE.

NC has announced the global version of AION 2 as a PC-focused release. Distribution is through Steam and PURPLE.

Is AION 2 global on console?

The official global-launch announcements currently describe Steam and PURPLE PC service. This page will be updated if NC announces additional platforms.

Global language support

NC previously said the global PC version is being prepared with support for ten languages, including English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.
