AION 2 Global Platforms: Steam & PURPLE

The AION 2 global version is a PC release distributed through Steam and PURPLE.

NC has announced the global version of AION 2 as a PC release distributed through Steam and PURPLE.

Steam

The global Steam release is part of NC's October 5 launch plan, with Early Access beginning September 30 for eligible players.

PURPLE

PURPLE is NC's own PC platform and is also included in the global rollout.

Is AION 2 global on console?

Current official global-launch announcements describe Steam and PURPLE PC service. GameHelpX will update this page if NC announces additional platforms.

Regions and language support

NC has announced service across North America, South America, Europe and Asia, with ten-language support planned for the global PC version.

Release timing

Early Access starts September 30, 2026, and the global formal launch follows on October 5.
