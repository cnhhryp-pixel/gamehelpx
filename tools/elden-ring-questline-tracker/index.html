Elden Ring Questline Tracker

Track which major NPC routes you are actively following without forcing one universal quest order.

Elden Ring questlines overlap with exploration and major progression, so this tool tracks routes independently rather than pretending they form one linear checklist.

Questlines

How to use it

Check a route when you consider it complete on the current character. For active routes, open the linked guide and note the last checkpoint separately if needed.

Why routes stay separate

NPC stories can overlap with bosses and regions but still have different requirements. Keeping them independent avoids creating a misleading “correct order.”
