Stardew Valley Bundle Checklist

Track Community Center room completion in your browser. Progress is saved locally on this device.

Use this checklist to track Community Center completion at the room level. It is intentionally simpler than an item-by-item tracker, making it useful as a quick dashboard for an active save.

How to use it

  1. Check a room only after its required bundles are complete.
  2. Use the main Bundles guide when you need room-specific planning.
  3. Reset the checklist when you start a new save or want to clear local progress.

Plan around seasonal items

Some bundle requirements depend on season, weather, fishing conditions or farm progression. A room-level tracker works best when paired with your crop, fishing and calendar pages.
