Elden Ring Equipment Load Planner

Add your equipped weight and maximum equip load to check the resulting movement-load category.

This planner compares total equipped weight with maximum equip load. It is useful when changing armor, weapons or talismans and trying to keep the same roll category.

Enter a loadout.

Load categories used

The planner uses the familiar equipment-load bands: below 30% for light load, 30% to below 70% for medium load, 70% to below 100% for heavy load, and 100% or more as overloaded.

Why this matters

A heavier weapon or armor piece can push the setup into a different movement category even when the build still meets all stat requirements.

Plan stats and equipment together

If the setup is too heavy, compare reducing equipment weight with adding Endurance. The Stat Planner helps show the opportunity cost of moving more levels into load support.
