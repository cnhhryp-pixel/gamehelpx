Elden Ring Boss Checklist

Track major progression encounters without treating Elden Ring as a strictly linear game.

This checklist focuses on the major progression route currently covered by GameHelpX. Elden Ring is non-linear, so it is a progress aid rather than a claim that every player must use one fixed order.

How to use it

  1. Check a boss after defeating it on the current character.
  2. Open the linked guide when you need preparation help.
  3. Leave alternate-route content outside this list until you actually pursue it.
  4. Reset the checklist when starting a new character.

What this tool does not do

It does not rank bosses or builds. Use it as a personal progress marker, then open Weapons, Talismans or the Stat Planner when the real problem is your loadout.
