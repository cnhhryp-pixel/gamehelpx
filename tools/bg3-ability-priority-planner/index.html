How to use the result

Your class and combat role should decide the primary ability. Constitution often matters as a survivability or concentration-support stat, while other abilities become more or less valuable depending on weapons, spellcasting and party skills.

Do not make every stat important

A build becomes easier to level when it has a clear priority order. Use the party to cover skills and roles your current character does not need to specialize in.
