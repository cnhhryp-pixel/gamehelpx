Why coverage and capacity differ

A layout can have enough theoretical sprinkler coverage but still lose useful crop tiles to overlap, paths or reserved space. Compare both numbers before planting permanently.

Use this with crop and processing plans

Greenhouse capacity only tells you how many crops fit. Use the Crop Planner and Artisan Value Calculator to decide what deserves that space.
