BG3 Concentration Loadout Checker

Spot competing concentration plans before a fight.

A character can normally maintain only one concentration spell at a time. This tool checks a planned set of effects and flags casters who have been assigned more than one.

Check the planned loadout.

What counts as a conflict

If two planned effects depend on concentration from the same caster, activating the second normally ends the first. Move one effect to another character or choose a non-concentration alternative.

Plan across the party

Concentration becomes easier to manage when each caster has one main persistent effect and several non-concentration actions to use around it.
