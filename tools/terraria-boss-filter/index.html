Terraria Boss Progression Filter

Filter the current GameHelpX boss guides by progression stage.

How to use the boss filter

Choose a progression stage to narrow the current GameHelpX boss guides. Use Pre-Hardmode when your world has not yet entered Hardmode, Transition for the Wall of Flesh milestone, Hardmode for mechanical and later bosses, and Endgame for the final main-progression encounter.

What this tool is for

The filter is designed to answer a simple question: “Which boss guide is relevant to my current stage?” It is not a rigid boss order. Terraria allows optional encounters and flexible preparation routes, so use the filtered list together with the main progression guide.

Recommended workflow

  1. Pick your current world stage.
  2. Open the boss you are preparing for.
  3. Check the related progression and equipment links on that guide.
  4. Return here after the fight to choose the next milestone.
