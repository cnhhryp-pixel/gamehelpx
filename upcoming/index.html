How GameHelpX rotates targets

A game gets homepage priority while it has an attractive search window. Once launch interest normalizes, its useful pages stay indexed for long-tail traffic while homepage attention moves to the next release.

We deliberately do not create fake walkthroughs before release. Pre-launch coverage is limited to confirmed release information, platforms, announced systems, FAQs and clearly labeled planned coverage.
